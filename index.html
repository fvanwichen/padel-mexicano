Rules

Fast rally scoring, doubles on every court. The match itself is identical in both formats — the only thing that changes is how partners are chosen each round.

Formats
Americano
The classic. You partner a different teammate every round and never play with the same person twice in a day, so by the end you've teamed up with everyone. The whole schedule is drawn at the start.
Mexicano
More competitive. Each round's pairings come from the live standings — the top players are grouped on one court, the chasing pack on another, and within each court the leader is paired with the lowest so games stay even. Win and you climb into tougher company. Only round 1 is random; every round after is earned.
The match
One rally, one point
No games, sets or 15–30–40. Every rally you win is worth exactly 1 point.
Winning the point
You win the rally when the other team can't return the ball legally — it bounces twice, goes into the net, or lands out. The glass walls are in play: after the ball bounces on the floor you can play it off your own back wall. Your own shot must clear the net and land in their court.
The serve
Underhand, struck at or below waist height, diagonally into the opposite box. You get two tries — miss both and the point goes to the other team.
Serving order
Decide who starts. The serve switches teams every 2 points, and when it comes back to your team the other partner takes it — so all four players serve equally. Serve from the right box first, then the left. Receivers keep their side for the whole match.
Reaching 24
Keep a running tally of rallies won. The match ends when the two scores add up to 24 — more points wins, 12–12 is a draw. Then enter the score on the Schedule screen.
